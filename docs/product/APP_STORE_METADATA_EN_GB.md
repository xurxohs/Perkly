## Description

```text
Perkly helps people in Uzbekistan discover useful local and digital offers, save favourites, activate promo codes and manage purchases in one app.

With Perkly you can:

• browse offers priced only in Uzbek soum (UZS);
• search by category, save favourites and get personalised recommendations;
• discover nearby offers on a map when you allow location access;
• follow a protected purchase flow and track order status;
• manage active and completed purchases, codes, gifts and history;
• chat with a seller or support, open disputes and submit reports;
• discover events and new places;
• create offers and promo codes as an approved business partner.

Location, camera and notification permissions are requested only when you use the relevant features. The catalogue and core features remain available without location access.

Perkly does not store payment card details. Balance top-ups are completed on the connected payment provider’s secure page.

Support: @perkly_support
```
